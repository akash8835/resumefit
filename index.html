        About ResumeFit
        A free resume coach built by a job seeker, for job seekers.
        ResumeFit started from a simple frustration: after dozens of job applications and very few replies, it was clear the resume was the problem - but nobody said exactly what to fix for each job. So we built a tool that does.
        What ResumeFit does
        • Scores your resume against a specific job and shows exactly what to fix, line by line
        • Rewrites it for that job and gives you a clean, ATS-safe PDF
        • Writes a cover letter, predicts interview questions, and estimates the salary range
        • Tracks your applications, keeps a resume version per role, and compares up to 5 jobs at once
        • Helps you find people who can refer you, and tunes your LinkedIn profile
        What we believe
        • Honest over impressive. ResumeFit never invents experience, numbers or skills. It only rephrases and re-orders what is really in your resume, and tells you plainly when a job needs something you do not have.
        • Free and no signup. Job hunting is stressful enough. You can use every feature without an account.
        • Your data stays yours. Saved resumes, history and your tracker live in your own browser, not on our servers. We only keep a resume if you tick the optional sharing box.
        Who is behind it
        ResumeFit is built and run by Akash Sharma, an aspiring product manager based in India. Feedback shapes what we build next - write to us at ashwatthama710@gmail.com or use the Contact us page.
        

        Privacy Policy
        What ResumeFit does with your information, in plain language.
        ResumeFit ("we", "us") is a free resume analysis tool at resumefit-app.ashwatthama710.workers.dev. This policy explains what information is processed when you use it. By using ResumeFit you agree to this policy.
        1. No accounts
        ResumeFit does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email or phone number to use the tool. You may separately choose to provide a mobile number or update email, as described below. Your resume itself may also contain contact details.
        2. Information you give us and how it is used
        • Resume content. When you upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file, the text is read inside your browser. When you run an analysis or another AI feature, that text is sent to our server to produce the result. A copy is also stored under the required sharing agreement described below.
        • Job descriptions and links. Job text you paste, or pages we fetch from a link you provide (for example LinkedIn or Naukri), are used only to produce your result.
        • Job searches. Search terms you type are sent to third-party job listing services to fetch matching jobs.
        • LinkedIn profile text you paste into the LinkedIn optimizer is used only to produce suggestions.
        • Anonymous usage statistics. When you run an analysis we keep a small anonymous record: the match score, the job title and company from the job description, the job keywords your resume was missing, the date, and your approximate country/region and device type. It contains no resume text, name or contact details, and is used only to understand how ResumeFit is used and improve it.
        • Required resume sharing. On the upload step there is a checkbox, unticked by default: "Share my resume with the ResumeFit team". You must tick it before uploading or using a saved resume. When you continue or upload in My resumes, we store a copy of the resume you continue with (its text and, where supported by the file size limit, the original file), the date and time you agreed, your approximate country/region and device type. Only the site owner can see these copies, and they are used only to review real resumes and improve ResumeFit. They are never sold, published or shared. To have your copy deleted, email us.
        • Optional contact, location and device details. Separate unchecked choices let you share a mobile number for resume-related contact, your browser-reported current coordinates and accuracy to understand where submissions come from, and device category, operating system and browser for troubleshooting. These choices do not affect your analysis. A separate Google Maps choice allows the coordinates to be sent to Google for an on-demand address lookup and map display. Address lookup does not make the captured location more accurate. Google responses are displayed on demand and are not saved in the resume database. Location requires browser permission and is captured once, not tracked continuously. Accuracy varies; coordinates and phone numbers are user/device supplied and are not verified. We do not automatically read your phone number. We store chosen details with the submitted resume, the consent wording version and the server receipt time; signed-in admins can view and export them. These details remain in browser memory only until submission or page close. Unchecking a choice stops it being sent with future submissions; it does not delete earlier records. Email us to request deletion.
        • Chrome extension downloads. Each time the extension zip is downloaded we log the date and time, the approximate country, region and city that Cloudflare derives from the connection, and the browser user agent (device and browser type). We do not store your IP address. We use this only to count downloads and see where they come from.
        • Optional update email. On the extension page you can leave your email to get ResumeFit updates. It is optional and the download works without it. If you give it, we store the email with the date, approximate region and device type, and use it only to send ResumeFit updates. To have it deleted, email us.
        3. AI processing
        Analyses, rewrites, cover letters, interview prep, salary estimates, LinkedIn suggestions and referral messages are generated by an AI model running on Cloudflare Workers AI. Your resume and job text are sent to that model to generate the response. Resume copies are stored under the required sharing agreement described above. We do not use your content to train AI models. AI output can be wrong or incomplete - always review it before you use it.
        4. Data stored on your device
        Saved resume versions, score history, your application tracker, and any LinkedIn Connections file you import are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Resume copies are also shared with our team as described above. Your imported connections are never sent anywhere. You can delete this data at any time from the app (Delete, Clear history, Remove) or by clearing your browser's site data.
        5. Third-party services
        • Google Maps receives coordinates only if you opt in to address lookup/map display. On supported native clients, the separate Google Geolocation endpoint accepts opted-in Wi-Fi/cell-tower signals; a normal webpage cannot scan those signals. See Google Privacy Policy.
        • Cloudflare hosts the app and runs the AI model. Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP address for security and reliability.
        • Job listing services (Remotive, Arbeitnow, Jooble, Himalayas, Jobicy, RemoteJobs.org) receive your search terms.
        • cdnjs serves the open-source libraries used to read PDF/DOCX files and create PDFs.
        • Pages you pull by link are fetched from the site you point to.
        Each service handles data under its own privacy policy.
        6. Cookies, analytics and ads
        ResumeFit does not use cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics, and does not show ads. The only records we keep are anonymous analysis statistics, the extension download log, optional update emails, and resumes and optional details you choose to share, as described in section 2, stored in our Cloudflare database and seen only by the site owner. If we add advertising or analytics in future (for example Google AdSense), we will update this policy first to explain what is collected and how to opt out.
        7. We do not sell your data
        We do not sell, rent or share your personal information, email or resume content with anyone for marketing.
        8. Security
        All traffic to ResumeFit uses HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so avoid including details you do not need on a resume, such as ID numbers or bank details.
        9. Children
        ResumeFit is meant for people aged 16 and over who are looking for work.
        10. Your choices
        You control your data: you choose what to upload, you can clear everything stored in your browser, and apart from the download log, any email you give and any resume you choose to share, nothing about you is kept on our servers. For any privacy question or request, contact us.
        11. Changes
        We may update this policy as the product changes. The version on this page is the one that applies.
        12. Contact
        Email ashwatthama710@gmail.com.
