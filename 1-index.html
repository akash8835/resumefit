      ResumeFit for Chrome
      One click on any LinkedIn or Naukri job page opens ResumeFit with that job already loaded and your saved resume ready.
      

      

      

      Install in under a minute
      

        

      1. Download the extension (.zip)
        2. 

      2. Unzip it into a folder you will keep.
        3. 

      3. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on Developer mode (top right).
        4. 

      4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
        5. 

      5. Open any LinkedIn or Naukri job. Click the blue Analyze with ResumeFit button.
        6. 

      

      

      

      How it works
      

        

      • Reads the job title, company and description from the page you are on.
        • 

      • Opens ResumeFit in a new tab with the job filled in. If you have a saved resume, it goes straight to the job step.
        • 

      • Works on linkedin.com/jobs and naukri.com job pages. Also available from the toolbar icon on any page with a job description.
        • 

      • No login, no tracking. The job text goes straight into the app in your browser.
        • 

      

      Save a resume in My resumes first so the one-click flow skips the upload step.
      

      

      

      

      


      Free, no signup. Your resume text is sent only to the AI model for analysis. Saved resumes, history, tracker and connections stay in your browser on this device.
